# Launch post draft

I’m opening up FocusLedger: a local-first Mac app that compares how you spent your
day with the goals you set in the morning.

It reads your active window with Apple Vision, uses Gemma through local Ollama to
estimate goal alignment, and generates a report when you choose View report or
End day. Screenshot pixels stay in memory; summaries stay on your Mac.

The public alpha includes a synthetic demo, documented setup, regression tests,
GitHub CI, an MIT license, and a contribution guide. It is a source-based early
release for Apple Silicon Macs; AI estimates can be wrong.

I’d love feedback on setup, accessibility, privacy controls, and the usefulness of
the daily reflection. Contributions are welcome.

Repository: https://github.com/Aaryan1524/FocusLedger
